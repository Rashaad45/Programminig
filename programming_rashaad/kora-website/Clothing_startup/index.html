( 01 ) — What we believe

We think most clothes are overdesigned and undermade. So we reversed it. Fewer seams, better thread, fabric you can hear when you fold it. Every piece is cut from cloth woven on handlooms within four hundred kilometres of where it is stitched — and every run stops at one hundred and eighty.
